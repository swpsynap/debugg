An auditor examines an array [b]A[/b] of [b]n[/b] signed 32-bit integers. Array positions are numbered from 1. Every non-empty contiguous segment [b]A&#91;L..R&#93;[/b] has a value residue (sum of its elements modulo 5) and a position residue (sum of its original positions [b]L[/b] through [b]R[/b] modulo 5).
A segment is index-aligned exactly when these two residues are equal and the number of values in the segment divisible by 3 is even. Count all index-aligned segments. Different endpoint pairs count separately, even when their values happen to match.

[b]Note:[/b][ul][li]A segment of length one may qualify, and the answer may be zero.[/li][li]The same array values can take part in several different segments; each segment is identified by its own endpoints.[/li][li]Residues are always taken in the range 0 to 4, also when the segment sum is negative.[/li][/ul]
