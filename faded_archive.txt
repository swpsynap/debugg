During storage, two non-overlapping pairs of consecutive original records were each replaced by a single record labelled ?, whose length is the sum of the two replaced lengths. Each ? stands for exactly two original records of positive length; their letters and lengths are lost. The two ? records may be adjacent.
You are given the stored records and the exact total number [b]C[/b] of A characters in the message. Count the distinct original messages that are consistent with the records, form a valid maximal-run representation and contain exactly [b]C[/b] letters A. Two reconstructions are counted separately only when their complete messages differ.
